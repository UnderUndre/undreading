#### **5.5.3. Echelon L2/L5: The Crypto-Agility Inventory [P0-22: PQC-INVENTORY] and Defense Against Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL)**

FIDO2 tokens close the authentication perimeter against instantaneous phishing. But the system's long-lived artifacts — company legal archives, wills, biometric and medical imagery (CBCT, DNA profiles), long-term multisig contracts — are exposed to a strategic threat: **"Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL)**.

Intelligence services and hostile groups intercept and warehouse encrypted traffic and backups on hard drives for years with one goal: **to decrypt them the moment quantum processors reach a sufficient count of stable qubits to run Shor's algorithm**.

##### **1. The NIST PQC standards (August 2024): the end of the RSA and ECC era**
In August 2024, the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) officially ratified the first final post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards:
* **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM):** A key-encapsulation mechanism on module lattices (*Module-Lattice Key Encapsulation Mechanism*, the CRYSTALS-Kyber algorithm). The full replacement for classical RSA and elliptic-curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) key exchange.
* **FIPS 204 (ML-DSA):** The module-lattice digital-signature standard (*CRYSTALS-Dilithium*). The replacement for ECDSA (secp256k1/Ed25519) and RSA.
* **FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA):** A hash-based, lattice-free digital signature (*SPHINCS+*). The backup asymmetric seal in case of a theoretical lattice break.

Per the **NIST IR 8547** directives and the US NSA's **CNSA 2.0** standard, classical algorithms (RSA-2048/4096, ECDSA, ECDH) are decommissioned from 2030 and fall under a **full statutory ban by 2035**.

##### **2. The mathematical risk assessment: Mosca's Theorem**
Dr. Michele Mosca formulated the mathematical criterion for the necessity of immediate migration to post-quantum protection:
$$T_L + T_M > T_Q$$
* $T_L$ (*Shelf Life*): how many years the data must stay secret (for DNA and family trusts — 30–50 years).
* $T_M$ (*Migration Time*): how many years the full audit and re-algorithmization of all system nodes will take.
* $T_Q$ (*Quantum Time*): when a quantum computer runs Shor's algorithm against 256-bit elliptic curves (forecast: 2030–2035).

If the sum of shelf life and migration time exceeds time-to-quantum — **your system is already in a state of compromise right now**. The data has been intercepted and is waiting for its hour.

* **Diversification protocol:**
  1. Hold **strictly $\le €100,000$ (or \$250,000 FDIC / £85,000 FSCS)** per unrelated banking license group.
  2. Channel surplus operating cash into short-term Treasury Bills (T-Bills) or non-custodial physical bullion in private vaulting (§11.3).
