### **🛠️ THE ROSETTA STONE: A DECODER FOR THE ENTRANCE GROUP'S "BIRD LANGUAGE"**
*(A Rosetta Stone for those who never served in special forces, never worked an ER, and never sued the IRS)*

Before you start turning valves, let's normalize all the codes into plain human language:

#### 1. Biology, medicine, and the body (Echelon L1)
* **L1 (Life / Biological echelon):** Your physical body, organs, vessels, and biochemistry. The foundation of the whole pyramid. If the aorta ruptures or the heart stops, your bitcoins and startups go to other people (probably).
* **N=1:** A statistics term for a sample of exactly one human being (the author's personal subjective experience, which you don't get to blindly extrapolate to all of humanity without lab verification).
* **TCCC (Tactical Combat Casualty Care):** The international military standard for casualty care on the battlefield. Built on the dry statistics of preventable deaths (90% of pre-hospital deaths are extremity bleeding, tension pneumothorax, and airway obstruction).
* **MARCH PAWS:** The stepwise combat-medicine sequence: **M**assive Bleeding (fountain-grade hemorrhage) $\to$ **A**irway $\to$ **R**espiration (chest wounds) $\to$ **C**irculation (pulse, secondary wounds) $\to$ **H**ypothermia $\to$ **PAWS** (pain relief, antibiotics, wounds, splints).
* **C-A-T Tourniquet (Combat Application Tourniquet Gen 7):** The modern army tourniquet with a plastic windlass. Applied one-handed in 10–15 seconds and guaranteed to occlude the brachial or femoral artery.
* **Esmarch bandage:** A 19th-century gray rubber strap with metal hooks. Freezes stiff and snaps in the cold, slips out of your hands in mud, and when applied badly crushes nerve trunks into limb paralysis. Modern tactical medicine classifies it as a dangerous anachronism.
* **BRAC (Basic Rest-Activity Cycle):** The 90-minute ultradian human rhythm discovered by Nathaniel Kleitman. The brain holds peak focus for about 90 minutes, then demands 15–20 minutes of decompression.
* **McGill Big 3:** Three isometric exercises (modified curl-up, side plank, bird-dog) developed by biomechanics professor Stuart McGill. They armor the lumbar corset without dangerous loaded flexion or spinal twisting.
* **Zone 2 (Zone 2 Cardio):** Low-heart-rate training (typically 110–135 bpm, when you can hold a full conversation without gasping). In this zone mitochondria burn fat at peak efficiency without lactate-acidifying your blood.
* **ApoB (Apolipoprotein B-100):** The most accurate modern blood test for atherosclerosis risk. It counts the absolute number of cholesterol-transporting particles penetrating vessel walls (unlike the outdated, inaccurate "total cholesterol").
* **CAC (Coronary Artery Calcium Score):** A CT-based calcium index of the heart's vessels. Shows how much hardened scale has already settled in your coronary arteries ($\text{CAC} = 0$ — a pristine clean riser; $\text{CAC} > 100$ — a building infarction threat).
* **Cystatin C:** A blood test for kidney filtration. Unlike standard creatinine, Cystatin C doesn't drift with muscle mass, meat intake, or gym supplements.
* **ACS (Acute Coronary Syndrome):** The pre-infarction state or a myocardial infarction already in progress (occlusion of a vessel feeding the heart).
* **DOAC (Direct Oral Anticoagulants):** Modern pill-form blood thinners (Eliquis/apixaban, Xarelto/rivaroxaban, Pradaxa/dabigatran).
* **Stroke:** Acute disruption of cerebral blood circulation.
* **BE FAST:** The mnemonic stroke test: **B**alance (sudden loss), **E**yes (double vision/blindness), **F**ace (facial droop), **A**rms (arm weakness), **S**peech (slurred speech), **T**ime (time to call emergency services).
* **LKW (Last Known Well):** The critically important timestamp: when the patient was last seen completely normal (the 4.5-hour thrombolysis window for dissolving a brain clot is counted from it).
* **NPO (Nulla per os):** The medical order "nothing by mouth." An absolute prohibition on giving water, food, or pills to a suspected stroke patient — a paralyzed swallowing reflex sends all of it straight into the lungs.
* **CWI (Cold Water Immersion):** Emergency immersion in ice water for heat stroke. The only method fast enough to cool a boiling brain before its proteins denature.
* **DenTek Temparin Max (US) / Cavit / Coltosol (UK):** Temporary filling material in a tube. Pressed into the hole from a lost filling with a finger or toothpick, it self-cures on contact with saliva within ~2 hours, sealing the exposed nerve off from infection until you reach a dentist.

#### 2. The digital perimeter and the brain (Echelon L2)
* **L2 (Liberty & Logic / Digital & Cognition):** Your software, cognitive hygiene, passwords, encryption, and attention defense.
* **SPOF (Single Point of Failure):** The node whose failure takes the entire system down at once (one password for everything, one bank, one client providing 90% of revenue).
* **Zero Push Policy:** The principle of total silence — push notifications fully disabled on phone and computer. You check messages when YOU scheduled the window, not when an algorithm yanks the dopamine leash.
* **FIDO2 / WebAuthn:** The international industry standard for passwordless site login via a physical USB hardware key (YubiKey) or device biometrics. Mathematically phishing-proof: the key validates the exact site address and will not sign a clone-domain request.
* **SMS-2FA (SMS Two-Factor Authentication):** Second-factor via text message. The worst defense on the market: the code is trivially intercepted through carrier SS7 holes or a plain SIM swap with a forged notarized request at a retail phone shop.
* **VeraCrypt / LUKS:** Tools for uncrackable encrypted containers or full-disk encryption. Without the master password, your drive reads as meaningless statistical noise.
* **KeePassXC / Aegis / 2FAS:** Open-source local password managers and code generators. Your entire vault lives on your own device in an encrypted file, not on a commercial company's servers waiting to be breached.
* **Syncthing (P2P E2EE):** Folder synchronization directly between your computers and phone over an encrypted peer-to-peer channel, bypassing third-party clouds like Google Drive or Dropbox.
* **NIST SP 800-61:** The US National Institute of Standards and Technology's cyber-incident response guide. The cardinal rule: if you're breached — do not reboot, pull the network cable, preserve volatile RAM for forensics, and rotate passwords from a different, clean device.
* **Shamir SSS (Shamir's Secret Sharing):** A mathematical algorithm for splitting a master password or key into $N$ shards. Example: split a key into 5 shards; any 3 restore access. No single person can steal your data.
* **AI Voice Cloning & SIP Spoofing:** Phone-fraud technologies: a neural net clones a relative's voice from 3 seconds of social-media audio, and the SIP telephony protocol forges the caller ID on your screen so it really looks like Mom or your brother is calling.
* **Safe Word (>75 bits of entropy):** A family-agreed passphrase of 4–5 random unrelated words (e.g., "*cast-iron radiator pedals a unicycle*") that cannot be brute-forced or guessed from social media. Required on any alarming money-transfer call.

#### 5. Hardware and the physical environment (Echelon L5)
